# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Job seekers, professionals, and recent graduates who need a standout, ATS-optimized portfolio website quickly. They are likely in the middle of a job hunt and need to present themselves professionally and dynamically without writing code.

## Product Purpose
"ProPortfolio" is an interactive SaaS application that instantly converts a user's uploaded Resume into a fully responsive, highly-customizable React + Tailwind CSS portfolio website. It also provides AI recruiter mock interviews. Success looks like users feeling confident in their generated portfolios and landing interviews.

## Brand Personality
Premium, high-converting, modern, sleek, and highly polished. It should feel like an elite SaaS product.

## Anti-references
- Cheap or generic resume template builders.
- Generic AI slop (overused Inter font without tuning, flat light-gray backgrounds, side-stripe cards).
- Overly cluttered enterprise dashboards.
- "Ghost-card" patterns (1px border + massive 16px blur shadow).

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence:** The UI must feel robust and "already finished" to inspire trust.
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Provide immediate visual feedback when building the portfolio (e.g., interactive preview).
- **Tactile and Fast:** Everything must respond instantly to interaction (proper easing, active states).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast (WCAG AA at minimum).
- Clear, descriptive focus states.
- Support for keyboard navigation.
